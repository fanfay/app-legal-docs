Privacy Policy for SlimPic

Last updated: May 25, 2026

SlimPic is a 100% local image compression tool.
All image processing happens entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. SlimPic processes all images locally on your device. No image data, usage data, or personal information is transmitted to our servers or any third party.

2. Photo Library Access

SlimPic requests access to your photo library only when you choose to import or save images. This access is used solely for the purpose you initiated — compressing and saving images. We never read, scan, or transmit your photo library contents.

3. No Third-Party Analytics

SlimPic does not use any third-party analytics services (such as Firebase Analytics, Amplitude, or Google Analytics). We do not track your behavior, device information, or usage patterns.

4. No Data Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties, service providers, or advertising networks, because we do not collect any data in the first place.

5. In-App Purchases

SlimPic offers in-app purchases (subscription and lifetime) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Apple's own privacy policy applies to payment processing. You can manage your subscription at Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

6. Data Retention

Since we do not collect any data, there is no data to retain. All processing results exist only on your device and are deleted when you remove them.

7. Children's Privacy

SlimPic does not knowingly collect information from children. Since no data is collected at all, our service is safe for users of all ages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
